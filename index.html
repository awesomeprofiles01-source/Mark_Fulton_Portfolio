Getting to Know Me

HR Q&A

How do you manage logistics in high-risk, resource-constrained environments?
By building systems before the pressure hits. In Kidal and Abyei, I developed supply chain processes, asset inventories, and contingency protocols ahead of time. When you're supporting EODD teams in an active conflict zone, a logistical failure has direct human consequences — that accountability sharpens every decision.
You've maintained a zero-incident record across two UN missions. How?
Safety outcomes are the result of culture, not luck. I built teams that understood why procedures mattered, not just what they were. I stayed close to the ground — regularly reviewing fleet maintenance, supply routes, and security briefings — so risks were identified before they became incidents.
How do you lead diverse, multinational teams under pressure?
With clarity and respect. In Mali I managed 50 local and international staff across languages and cultures. The foundation is always the same: be transparent about expectations, give people real ownership, and communicate consistently. People perform when they understand the mission and trust their leader.
Why did you build OpsTrainer while seeking field work?
Because I've seen what poorly trained field workers cost — in efficiency, safety, and lives. OpsTrainer is a long-term mission I'm committed to, but it doesn't replace the pull I feel toward field operations. I'm building something that will outlast any single deployment, while staying available for the work I know best.
What's your approach to cost efficiency on mission?
I reduced budget waste by 20% in Mali not through cuts, but through visibility. When you know exactly where every resource is and what it costs, waste becomes obvious. I built custom tracking systems that gave my team and our clients real-time financial transparency — and that changed how decisions were made.
What are you looking for in your next role?
A field operations or logistics role where the stakes are real and standards are high. I thrive in complex, multi-stakeholder environments — UN missions, large NGO operations, emergency response. I want to contribute to a team that values operational rigour and the human dimension of humanitarian work.
